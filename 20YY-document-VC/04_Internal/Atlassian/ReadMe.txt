As the main billing or technical contact for your Bitbucket workspace, you should already receive copies of your monthly invoice for bitbucket.org/<workspace> (https://bitbucket.org/<workspace>/workspace/overview/) directly to your registered email address, as per your request.

Currently, Bitbucket billing and invoicing are managed separately from Jira and other Atlassian products. This is why you only see Jira project invoices under your Atlassian Admin Billing Preview (https://admin.atlassian.com/o/xxxxxxxxxxxxx/billing-preview).

Here’s how you can access and download your quotes or invoices:

You can log into your My Atlassian account (https://www.atlassian.com/purchase/my/) and download quotes and invoices, make payments, add PO numbers, delete quotes, and make changes to existing/open quotes.

Log into your My Atlassian account (https://www.atlassian.com/purchase/my/) and click the Orders tab in the navigation bar.

    In the Quotes tab, you can download a PDF quote or invoice, make a credit card payment, add a PO number, make changes to the quote, or delete the quote altogether.

    In the Invoices tab, you can see an order's details by clicking the >, or download a PDF of the paid invoice by clicking the Invoice link in the Actions tab.
